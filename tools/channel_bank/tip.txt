// Tips: short, useful, true for real pool and for the game. They bring people back and build trust.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: sindirishda oq sharni nazoratda saqlang
Boshlang'ich zarbada eng kuchli urish shart emas. Oq shar uchburchakka to'g'ri tegsin va stol o'rtasida qolsin: shunda keyingi zarbangiz oson bo'ladi.
en: 💡 Tip: keep the cue ball under control on the break
You do not have to hit the break with full force. Let the cue ball meet the rack squarely and stay near the middle of the table, and your next shot is easy.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: oson sharlardan boshlang
Qiyin sharni birinchi urmang. Avval oson sharlarni tushiring, oq sharni keyingi qiyin shar yoniga olib keling. Natijada stolda faqat oson zarbalar qoladi.
en: 💡 Tip: start with the easy balls
Do not shoot the hard ball first. Pocket the easy ones and bring the cue ball next to the harder one. You end up with only easy shots on the table.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: 8-sharni oxiriga qoldiring, lekin yo'lini oldindan tozalang
O'z guruhingiz oxirgi shari tushganda 8-shar cho'ntakka ochiq yo'lda turishi kerak. Oxirgi 2–3 zarbadan oldin 8-shar yo'lida to'siq bormi, tekshiring.
en: 💡 Tip: leave the 8-ball for last, but clear its path early
When your last group ball drops, the 8-ball needs a clear path to a pocket. Before your last 2-3 shots, check whether anything blocks it.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: yo'l to'silsa, bortdan aylaning
Raqibning shari yo'lingizni to'ssa, to'g'ridan-to'g'ri urishga urinmang: oq sharni bortga uring (kick shot). Burchak kirish burchagiga teng qaytadi.
en: 💡 Tip: if the path is blocked, go round the cushion
When an opponent's ball blocks you, do not force the direct shot: send the cue ball off a cushion (a kick shot). The angle out equals the angle in.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: ingichka zarbada kuchni pasaytiring
Nishon sharni faqat chetidan ilashtirsangiz (ingichka kesim), kuchli urmang. Sekin zarba aniqroq bo'ladi va oq shar ham uzoqqa ketmaydi.
en: 💡 Tip: soften the power on a thin cut
When you only clip the edge of the object ball (a thin cut), do not hit hard. A slow shot is more accurate and the cue ball does not run away.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: oq sharning keyingi o'rnini o'ylang
Zarba tushdi, endi oq shar qayerda to'xtaydi? Pastdan urish (draw) uni orqaga tortadi, yuqoridan urish (follow) oldinga yuritadi. Mashq rejimida ikkalasini sinab ko'ring.
en: 💡 Tip: think about where the cue ball will stop
The ball dropped, but where does the cue ball end up? Hitting low (draw) pulls it back, hitting high (follow) sends it forward. Try both in Practice mode.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: oq sharni bort yoniga qoldirmang
Bort yonidagi oq shar bilan zarba qiyinlashadi. Sharni stolning o'rtasiga yaqin qoldirishga harakat qiling: u yerdan hamma tomonga yo'l ochiq.
en: 💡 Tip: do not leave the cue ball against the cushion
A cue ball hugging the cushion makes the next shot hard. Try to leave it near the middle of the table: from there every direction is open.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: kombinatsiyani qidiring
Ikki shar bir-biriga yaqin turganda birinchisini ursangiz, u ikkinchisini cho'ntakka itarishi mumkin. Bunday zarba kam uchraydi, lekin raqibni hayratda qoldiradi.
en: 💡 Tip: look for the combination
When two balls sit close together, hitting the first can push the second into a pocket. Such shots are rare, but they impress your opponent.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: har zarbada vaqt bor
Navbatingiz boshlanishi bilan taymer yuradi. Zarbani oldindan rejalashtiring: raqib zarba urayotganda keyingi sharlaringizni hisoblab qo'ying.
en: 💡 Tip: every shot has a clock
The timer runs as soon as your turn starts. Plan the shot ahead: work out your next balls while your opponent is shooting.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: avval o'z guruhingiz sharini uring
Oq shar birinchi bo'lib raqibning sharini yoki 8-sharni ursa, qoida buzilishi bo'ladi va raqib oq sharni xohlagan joyga qo'yadi. Nishonni diqqat bilan tanlang.
en: 💡 Tip: hit your own group first
If the cue ball touches an opponent's ball or the 8-ball first, it is a foul and your opponent can place the cue ball anywhere. Choose your target carefully.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: oq shar qo'lingizda bo'lsa, ikki zarbani rejalashtiring
Oq sharni xohlagan joyga qo'yish imkonini bersa, faqat bitta sharni emas, keyingi sharni ham o'ylang: oq shar birinchi zarbadan keyin ikkinchisiga qulay joyda to'xtasin.
en: 💡 Tip: with the cue ball in hand, plan two shots
When you may place the cue ball anywhere, think about the next ball too, not just one: let the cue ball finish in a good spot for the second shot.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: har kuni 5 daqiqa mashq
Mashq rejimida bitta qiyin zarbani 10 marta takrorlang. Bir haftadan keyin qiyin zarba odatiy bo'lib qoladi. Bunda tanga ham, reyting ham xavf ostida emas.
en: 💡 Tip: 5 minutes of practice a day
In Practice mode repeat one hard shot 10 times. After a week the hard shot becomes routine. Neither coins nor ranking are at stake.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: aylanishni oz-ozdan qo'shing
Yon aylanish (English) oq shar yo'lini o'zgartiradi. Boshlang'ich darajada oz miqdorda ishlating, aks holda zarba aniqligi buziladi. Avval Mashq rejimida kuzating.
en: 💡 Tip: add spin little by little
Side spin ("English") bends the cue ball's path. Use a small amount at first, or your accuracy suffers. Watch it in Practice mode first.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: nishon chizig'ini barmog'ingiz bilan to'smang
Telefonda nishonni aniq ko'rish uchun barmoqni chizig'ingiz yonidan uzoqroq tuting. Aniq nishon yarim g'alaba.
en: 💡 Tip: do not hide the aim line with your finger
On a phone, keep your finger away from the aim line so you can see it clearly. A clear aim is half the win.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: qiyin zarbadan oldin xavf va foydani solishtiring
Qiyin zarba urilmasa, raqibga qulay holat qoladi. Foydasi kichik, xavfi katta bo'lsa, himoya (safety) tanlang: oq sharni raqib sharlari orqasiga yashiring.
en: 💡 Tip: weigh risk against reward before a hard shot
A missed hard shot leaves your opponent a good position. If the reward is small and the risk is big, play safe: hide the cue ball behind your opponent's balls.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: butun tangangizni bitta o'yinga sarflamang
Stolni hamyoningizga mos tanlang. Yutqazish ham o'yinning bir qismi: tangangiz bir necha o'yinga yetsin, shunda ruhiy bosim kam bo'ladi va zarbalar aniqroq chiqadi.
en: 💡 Tip: do not put all your coins on one match
Choose a table that fits your wallet. Losing is part of the game: if your coins last for several matches, there is less pressure and your shots come out cleaner.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: kuchli raqib oldida sabr qiling
Kuchli raqib ham xato qiladi. Shoshilmang, oddiy zarbalarni ishonch bilan uring va uning xatosini kuting. Ko'pincha o'yinni sabrli o'yinchi yutadi.
en: 💡 Tip: be patient against a strong opponent
Even a strong opponent makes mistakes. Do not rush, play the simple shots with confidence and wait for the slip. Often the patient player wins.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: sportcha o'ynang
O'yin tugagach «Yaxshi o'yin» deb yozing, yurak yuboring. G'alabada ham, mag'lubiyatda ham hurmat saqlang: shunda raqiblar siz bilan yana o'ynashni xohlaydi.
en: 💡 Tip: be a good sport
After the match type "Good game" and send a heart. Keep your respect in a win and in a loss: that way opponents want to play you again.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: ko'zingizni asrang
Bir soat o'ynagach 5 daqiqa tanaffus qiling: uzoqqa qarang, suv iching. Ko'z va qo'l dam olsa, zarbalar aniqroq bo'ladi. O'yin dam olish uchun, charchash uchun emas.
en: 💡 Tip: look after your eyes
After an hour of play take a 5-minute break: look into the distance, drink some water. Rested eyes and hands shoot better. The game is for relaxing, not for wearing yourself out.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: yangi o'yinchi uchun yo'l
London → Sidney → Lissabon: avval birinchi uzukni yig'ing (4 ta g'alaba), keyin keyingisini. Har uzuk yangi stol va yangi kubokni ochadi.
en: 💡 Tip: a path for new players
London → Sydney → Lisbon: collect the first ring first (4 wins), then the next one. Each ring opens a new table and a new cup.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: kunlik kubokka oldindan tayyorlaning
Kubok ochilishidan 10 daqiqa oldin Mashq rejimida qizinib oling. Ochilgach 3 soat bor, lekin tayyor bo'lsangiz, birinchi raundda ham xato qilmaysiz.
en: 💡 Tip: warm up before the daily cup
Warm up in Practice mode 10 minutes before the cup opens. You have 3 hours once it does, but if you are ready you will not slip even in round one.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: bir zarbada ikki shar
Ikki shar bir-biriga yaqin bo'lsa va cho'ntak oldida bo'lsa, ikkalasini bitta zarbada tushirish mumkin. «Ajoyib o'yin» medalini shunday olasiz: ichida 1 000 🪙 bor.
en: 💡 Tip: two balls with one shot
If two balls are close together near a pocket, you may drop both with one shot. That is how you earn the "Great game" medal, with 1,000 🪙 inside.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: sindirishda shar tushiring
Birinchi zarbada shar tushirsangiz, «Tezkor» medalini olasiz. Oq sharni uchburchak uchi bilan to'g'ri ro'para qiling va o'rtacha kuchli uring.
en: 💡 Tip: pot a ball on the break
Pot a ball on the break and you earn the "Quick" medal. Line the cue ball up squarely with the head ball and hit with moderate power.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: sharning qaysi qismiga urishni biling
Nishon shar to'g'ridan-to'g'ri cho'ntak tomon ketishi uchun, oq shar uning cho'ntakka qarama-qarshi tomoniga tegishi kerak. Xayoliy shar usuli shu yerda yordam beradi.
en: 💡 Tip: know where to hit the ball
For the object ball to head straight for the pocket, the cue ball must touch it on the side opposite the pocket. The ghost-ball method helps here.

## tip tips
uz: 💡 Maslahat: kichik maqsadlar qo'ying
Bugun 3 ta g'alaba, ertaga uzuk, indinga yangi shahar. Kichik maqsadlar yo'lni qisqartiradi va har kuni sizni o'yinga qaytaradi. Maqsadingiz nima?
en: 💡 Tip: set small goals
Today 3 wins, tomorrow a ring, the day after a new city. Small goals shorten the road and bring you back every day. What is yours?
